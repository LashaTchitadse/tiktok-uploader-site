Privacy Policy

Home · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

  • Data accessed. The tool accesses the authorizing TikTok user's basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and uploads the video files that the user chooses.
  • Storage. OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept only on the owner's computer, in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is stored on any server operated by this tool, because no such server exists.
  • Sharing. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs, and only to perform the upload the user requested. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Deletion. Revoke the app's access in TikTok's settings and delete the tokens from Keychain. Once that is done, the tool holds no data.
