Uploader Asst

Home · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Uploader Asst is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and uses the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account, either as drafts or as posts.

It is not a public service. It does not have other users, it does not sell anything, and it does not collect data from anyone else.
